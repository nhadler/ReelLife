# Progress, Pace and Deadlines

## The Header

The project header shows how much is **Composed** (cues and time) and
what **Remains**, the daily pace, the next deadline and how many cues
are on older picture. **Breakdown** shows the detailed totals:
total music and how much is Score, Source and Song, music to license,
time in each status (Approved, Composed, Revisions, In Progress,
Rewrite, To Write), what remains, and the daily pace. ReelLife remembers
whether you leave it open or closed. The bar under the header shows the
same statuses.

Progress counts compositions only, unless App Settings, **Progress
Counts** includes songs and licensed music. Omitted and on-hold cues
never count.

## Per Day

**Per day** is the music still to write (To Write and In Progress), and
to revise (Revisions and Rewrite), spread evenly over the days from
today, or from the deadline's start date if it is later, through the
composition deadline, including both dates and excluding days off. It
rounds up to the next second.
Stats lets you count down to another deadline instead.

## Days Off

- **Your days off** (in your picture's menu, or on New Project) apply to
  every project: holidays, vacation.
- **This project's days off** (Project Settings, Deadlines) apply only
  to that project, such as days when its studio is closed.

Click days on the calendar to add or remove them; Shift-click marks a
range. **Today** brings the calendar back to this month.

## Stats

**Stats** (Ctrl+Shift+S) opens beside the grid: totals, progress, pace,
deadlines, and time by status, cue type, sub status, theme and
instrumentation. Click a figure to select or filter those cues.
