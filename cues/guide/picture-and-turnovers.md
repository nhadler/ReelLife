# Picture and Turnovers

## Picture types

Chosen when you make a project, and changeable in Settings, Picture:

- **Reels**: numbered reels (1, 2, 3).
- **Acts**: numbered acts, for television.
- **Clips**: names of letters and numbers (A12, B3).
- **Long Play**: one continuous picture with no reels. Its turnovers
  ask only for IN and OUT.

Labels across ReelLife follow the type: *Reel 1*, *Act 2*, *Clip A12*,
or just *Picture*. Settings won't switch to a type the existing pictures
don't fit (say, Reels while clips are named A12).

## Log a turnover

When a new cut arrives:

1. Open **Picture** in the project bar and choose **Log a Turnover**.
2. Give its label (`c1030`, `LOCK`) and the date it arrived.
3. Tick the reels that changed and type their new IN and OUT. Reels you
   leave unticked keep their current picture.
4. Add any **new reels** with their names, IN and OUT.
5. Choose **Log Turnover**.

Each reel's *current picture* is the newest turnover that includes it.
If a reel's IN moved, ReelLife opens **Conform** next (see *Conform*).

Click a turnover's name on the Picture page to change it, or to delete
it. A reel that cue versions were written to can't be dropped, and such
a turnover can't be deleted.

## Cues not on a reel yet

Cues imported before any reel had timecodes sit on *No Reel Yet*. Once
a turnover gives the reels timecodes, the Picture page lists those cues
under **Cues Not on a Reel**, with the reel each one's IN falls in.
Choose **Place Cues** to move them, in one step you can undo.
