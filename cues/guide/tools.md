# Cue Tools

The **Tools** menu (Q) acts on the selected cues. Each shows a preview
first, and each run is one change that Undo takes back.

- **New Cues**: several cues in one reel, numbered in order.
- **Add Versions**: the next version for each selected cue (or every cue
  shown), keeping notes and sub status if you like.
- **Split Cue**: one cue into two at a timecode, with a new number.
- **Merge Cues**: two or more into one, IN from the first, OUT from the
  last.
- **Renumber Cues**: new numbers from a starting cue #, keeping
  suffixes like `a` or `s` if you like, and optionally the reel or the
  timecode hour in the number.
- **Shift Cues**: earlier or later by an offset (`+2:12`, `-00:00:01:00`)
  or to a new IN.
- **Move Cues** / **Copy Cues**: to another reel.
- **Delete Cues** (Ctrl+Shift+⌫).
- **Undo Last Change** (Ctrl+Z).
