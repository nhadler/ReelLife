# Credits and Cue Sheets

## Credits

**Credits** in the top bar is one list of writers and publishers for
all projects: names (writers have first and last names), PRO, IPI/CAE
number, and whether it's the composer's own. A writer or publisher
still credited on a cue can't be removed. **Undo Last Change** takes
back your latest save there, and Recent Changes lists the last few.
Export the list as CSV or Excel.

## A cue's shares

On a cue's page, **Credits** lists its writers and publishers with
their roles (C composer, A author, CA, AR arranger, E publisher...) and
shares. Writer shares add up to 100%, and publisher shares too when
there are any. **Add Row** adds more lines. If someone else saved the
same cue's credits meanwhile, ReelLife shows theirs and lists what you
had, rather than overwriting them.

## Default shares

Project Settings, Cue Sheet has the project's **default shares**, given
to every new composition (not Song or Source cues). **Apply** gives them
to existing cues too.

## The cue sheet

Fill in Project Settings, Cue Sheet (production company, category,
preparer...). Then **Export Cue Sheet** (Settings menu, or Tools):
RapidCue Excel or CSV, optionally a revised sheet, cue # before each
title, IPI numbers, notes, artist, and inactive cues. Before the file,
the **checklist** shows what a PRO would query: missing titles, lengths
or writers, shares that don't add up, writers without a PRO, cues not
composed yet or still on older picture.
