# Conform

After a new cut, cues need to follow the picture. **Conform** (Settings
menu, or the "on older picture" link in the header) has two parts.

## Reels That Moved

When a reel's head changes and nothing else does, every cue in it moves
by the same amount. Conform lists each reel whose IN moved in the latest
turnover, with how far; tick the reels and choose **Shift Cues**. A
shift applies once: the page then shows the reel as already shifted.
Cues already placed on the new cut stay where they are.

## On Older Picture

Cues whose current version was written to an earlier turnover are
listed with their start offset and change in length. Either re-import
from your DAW (the import preview shows the offset), or tick them
(**Select All** ticks every one) and **Mark Conformed**:

- *Moving the Current Version to the New Picture* when the music still
  fits, or
- *Adding a New Version on the New Picture*, which keeps the notes.

Shifting and marking are each one change you can undo.
