# Cues and Versions

## Add cues

- **New Cue** (Ctrl+N; ⌃N on a Mac) opens a dialog: cue #, title, type,
  status, reel, IN and OUT, notes. **Save and Add Another** keeps going.
- **Tools, New Cues** adds several in one reel at once.
- For a large cue list, import from your DAW (see *Importing*).

A cue # must fit the project's **Valid Cue Numbers** (Settings, Cue
Sheet): the standard numbers (`1m01`, `1m04a`, `1s01`, `101m01`), any
number, or your own pattern. This rule applies to new cues, renames,
tools, restores from Trash and imports. Existing numbers that don't
match are kept until you change them.

## Edit in the grid

- Click a cell to edit it (or double-click, if you turned single-click
  editing off in App Settings). Enter or Tab saves. In notes cells,
  Enter adds a line or saves, depending on App Settings; Shift+Enter
  always adds a line and Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter) always saves.
- Each save is one change, so **Undo** (Ctrl+Z) takes it back.
- Edit several cues at once: select them (tick their boxes; Shift-click
  for a range), then change one cell. ReelLife asks whether to apply it
  to all of them.
- Select all shown: Ctrl+Shift+A. Deselect: Ctrl+Shift+D. Invert:
  Ctrl+Shift+I. Copy selected cells: Ctrl+C.
- Click a reel's row, or press Enter on it, to collapse or expand its cues.

## Find and arrange

- **Filter** (F) by status, cue type, reel, priority and more; **Show**
  (Shift+1 to Shift+6) picks active, inactive and so on. Ctrl+Shift+X
  clears the filters.
- The search box (Ctrl+F) matches cue numbers, titles, the scene and
  every notes column (spotting, notes, revision, conform, temp, IN and
  OUT, scene description and the department notes) of each cue's
  current version, including hidden columns. Every word you type must match
  somewhere: `door storm` finds cues that mention both.
- **Views** are the tabs along the bottom (Standard, Spotting, Conform
  and more; Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9). **View** (V) changes columns and row
  size. Column order, widths and sorting are saved to the view for
  everyone. Ctrl+Shift+= creates a view.
- The timeline above the grid shows each reel's cues; click one to find
  it in the grid, or drag it to another lane.

## Versions

A cue's version is one delivery of its music (v1, v2, v1.1...).

- The **+** beside the version in the grid, or Ctrl+Shift+V, opens
  **New Version**: label, the picture it's written to, timecodes,
  notes. It starts with the current version's notes. A new version of
  an Approved cue goes back to Composed (review reopens), unless you
  pick another status.
- **Tools, Add Versions** adds the next version to each selected cue.

## Cue details

**Cue Details** (Ctrl+Enter, or a click on the thumbnail) opens a cue's
page: every field, its versions, credits (writers and publishers),
how it compares with the current picture, and its **History**. History
lists each change and who made it. **Revert** undoes a change when nothing
later depends on it. Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ go to the previous and next cue.
